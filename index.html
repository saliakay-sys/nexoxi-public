Proposed Stellar / Soroban Integration

NEXOXI is exploring a focused Soroban integration for verifiable service attestations.

The proposed approach combines Oracle Mind evidence assessment and VouchTrust provider attestations with permission-bounded coordination.

The goal is to allow Stellar-based applications to record and independently verify authorized service evidence without placing sensitive personal or confidential information on-chain.

This integration is proposed and has not yet been implemented or deployed.

No existing Stellar partnership, grant approval, or operational Soroban integration is claimed.
